Act 01 · Set up

Setting up an inspection
has never been simpler.

Plan ahead from the web platform, or create it on-site in the app — tap the +, fill only what's needed. The draft planned at the office switches to the field with a single tap.

Act 02 · Configure

Simplified
configuration.

Default settings are available for each property type. Studio, 1BR to 5BR, house: select the type, all rooms are created in one click — living room, bedrooms, kitchen, bathroom, WC, ready to inspect.

Act 03 · Fill in

Fill in
with ease.

Become a real photographer, the app handles the rest. Whether by photo or voice input, capture every element of the rooms — in-depth photo analysis writes a precise description, section by section, without you touching the keyboard.

Act 04 · Sign

Review,
then sign.

Share the summary with your tenants, get it validated, then launch the signatures to confirm the inspection and share it instantly. Everything is compliant, traceable, and in the mailbox before you leave.

FusionFox · the breakthrough that's coming and the simplicity you've been waiting for

We already invented
what comes next.

FusionFox is coming. No more photos to take, no more descriptions to validate. A full room scan — and the inspection exists. All you need to do is review it.

The revolution, in 3 steps
01
3 → 10 min Full scan
You turn the iPhone around the apartment.
Room by room, LiDAR captures every square centimeter — walls, floor, ceiling, furniture, defects. Studio: 3 minutes. 3BR: 6 minutes. House: 10 minutes max. No photos to take, no dictation. You walk, the scan follows.
02
5 min Generation
The system processes the scan and writes the inspection report.
Automatic identification of rooms, materials, defects. Centimeter-precise measurements. Written descriptions, states assigned, photos extracted from useful angles. In 5 minutes, the full report is ready — far more than a raw scan.
03
All that's left Review
You validate. You sign.
You browse what the scan saw. Correct what needs to be corrected with one tap, keep the rest. The machine proposes, the human decides. No surprises when it's time to sign — your eye remains the final filter.
⚡ Included bonus

Re-rented before they even leave.

The scan automatically generates a shareable 3D virtual tour. Your prospects view it from their couch, make their decision before the property is free. You re-rent faster, with no extra trips — rental vacancy drops to zero.
